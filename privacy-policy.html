Controluce

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. In short

Controluce needs no account. Your photos, birth details and readings stay on your phone. Something is sent only when you ask Luigi for a text, and only if you have agreed. There are no ads and no tracking.

2. What is sent

For a hand reading: the photos of your hands, reduced in size and without location data, your answers to the questions about your hands, and a summary of your birth chart if you gave your birth details. For horoscope texts: the summary of your birth chart and, for some of them, your answers to Luigi and a few lines from your latest reading. No photos. For a reading for two, the other person's chart too: ask them first. Your name never leaves your phone.

3. Who writes the texts

The texts are written by Anthropic, a US artificial-intelligence company. Under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models, and as a rule it deletes it within 30 days.

4. What we keep

Requests pass through us on their way to Anthropic. We keep no photos and no texts: only simple counters, linked to a random code, to limit how often texts can be requested. They delete themselves after about two months.

5. Your consent and your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time in the App's settings: from then on, nothing is sent. You can access, correct or delete your data; since almost everything is on your phone, you can do it from the App itself. For anything else, write to us. You can also contact your data protection authority.

6. Adults only

Controluce is for people aged 18 or over.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
