Write to support@bottega01.com. A person reads and answers. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version.

Frequently asked

How do I take good photos?

Open hand, fingers straight, hand still and entirely inside the screen. When the outline turns green the photo is good, but you can take it anyway.

Why does a reading take a while?

Luigi writes the whole reading in one go: it can take a couple of minutes. Keep the App open.

Why does Luigi ask for another photo?

Some parts of the hand only show with light from the side, or from the back. You can say "not now": the rest of the reading stays the same.

Do I need my birth details?

No. Without them Luigi reads only your hands. With them he also reads your birth chart, and the time of birth makes it more precise.

Will my readings move to a new iPhone?

Not through a backup: they stay on this phone and are not included in backups.

How do I delete my data?

In the App you can delete single photos and readings, or everything at once from the settings.
